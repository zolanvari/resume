LinkedIn caption - cv.zolanvari.com launch
============================================

I got tired of résumé builders that cost $20/month - so I built a free one
and open-sourced it.

cv.zolanvari.com is live. Upload your old résumé - PDF, Word, or just pasted
text - and Gemini reads it, extracts the structure, and typesets it into a
clean, professional PDF. Seven themes, AI bullet-polish, one-click export.

Four things I cared about:
→ Shipped & hosted - a real, running product, not a demo screenshot.
→ Genuinely useful - it produces a résumé you'd actually send.
→ Free - no paywall, no "upgrade to download," no account.
→ Open source - fork it, file issues, send PRs.

Under the hood: Gemini 2.5 on GCP for extraction, Typst for typesetting,
React + FastAPI. Résumés are processed transiently and never stored unless
you explicitly opt in.

Layoffs are everywhere right now, and a good résumé shouldn't sit behind a
subscription. So here's one that doesn't.
