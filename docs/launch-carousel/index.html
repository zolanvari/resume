The problem

Most résumé tools want your money, your email, and your data.

“Sign up to continue.” “Upgrade to download.” Meanwhile your résumé - full of personal details - sits on someone else's servers. When you're job-hunting under pressure, that's the last thing you need.

The project · cv.zolanvari.com

A résumé builder that's free, open source, and stores nothing.

Upload your old résumé as a PDF or Word file. AI reads it and organises it into clean, structured fields - no account, no fee. It's processed in the moment and discarded; nothing is kept unless you explicitly opt in.
